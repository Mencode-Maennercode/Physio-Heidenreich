The idea

Therapy that adapts to your life – not the other way round.

You stay at home. I bring everything the treatment needs and work in the surroundings you move through anyway. That makes progress visible in daily life straight away.

01

Comfort at home

Mobile physiotherapy right where you live: flexible, individual and without the stress. Your treatment fits around your day, not the other way round.

02

Attentive care

One consistent contact, a calm arrival, full confidentiality. Family can be involved if you wish.

03

Time and expertise

Every treatment gets the time it needs – and is documented, so your progress stays traceable. Focus on neurology, deepened through continuous professional development.

Treatments

One focus, one treatment plan

Every course of treatment begins with a thorough assessment. From it comes a plan that fits your condition, your pace and your home.

KI

Neurological treatment

After a stroke, with Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis or polyneuropathy: walking, balance, fine motor skills and safety in daily life.

KI

Orthopaedic treatment

Targeted mobilisation of joints and tissue for complaints in the back, shoulder, hip and knee – including post-operative care.

KI

Fall prevention

Strength, balance and steadiness – including a look at trip hazards in your home and exercises for the week.

KI

Lymphatic drainage

Gentle manual technique to stimulate lymph flow with swelling after surgery and with lymphoedema, with bandaging where needed.

KI

Kinesio taping

Elastic tape to relieve muscles and joints – supportive for complaints and for stability in daily life.

About me

Nora Heidenreich

Physiotherapist (B.Sc. NL) | M.A. (Health Administration)

I studied physiotherapy in the Netherlands and worked for many years in university hospitals, mostly in neurology. That is where I saw how much of a treatment is lost to travelling, waiting and time pressure.

So I come to my patients at home – with fixed appointments, enough time and a deliberately small caseload. If you wish, I involve family members so that exercises can continue safely between appointments.
